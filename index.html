Learn Qur'anic Words

Study the most common words of the Qur'an, then test yourself. Pick what you want to work on and start.

How to Use

1. Learn

On the home page, choose Learn, pick one or more categories, and press Start Learning! You'll see each Arabic word with its meaning. When you feel ready, press Practice these words.

2. Practice

Choose Practice, pick your categories, and press Start Quiz! You'll get a card for each word.

  • Type the English meaning in the card's box and press Enter.
  • Right answers turn green and you move to the next card automatically.
  • Wrong answers turn red and show the meaning. If your answer was actually fine (for example, a different wording), press ✓ I was right.
  • Press Finish Quiz at the bottom to see your score and retry the words you missed.

Tips

  • Only the most common meaning is listed. Many words have others depending on context.
  • "to", "the", and "a" are ignored, and small typos are forgiven.
  • The small gray note on a card (like "fem.") tells you which form it is.

How It Works

Because high-frequency vocabulary repeats so often throughout the Qur'an, progress isn't linear: it climbs rapidly at the beginning!

  • First ~120 words: Covers function words like pronouns and prepositions (41.1% of the entire text).
  • Next ~230 words: Adds essential thematic nouns like prophets, signs, and faith (brings you to 60%).
  • Next ~600 words: Covers primary 3-letter verbs and derived forms (surpasses 85% coverage).

About

Words of Qur'an is 100% free and open source. There are no ads, accounts, or paywalls, and it will stay that way.

It's a practice tool for learning the most frequent words of the Qur'an. When I was studying Japanese, I used Tofugu's Kana Quiz to quiz myself on each letter. It was way more fun than just reading and taking notes, and I retained information much better. Later, I wanted to understand more of the Qur’an without always reading the translation. Then I found a pdf from understandquran.com, and thought it would be more fun to get instant feedback via a quiz so it would feel more like a game, and I figured other people might enjoy using it too.
