The 99 Names of Allah

"Allah has ninety-nine names, one hundred less one. Whoever memorizes them will enter Paradise." (Sahih al-Bukhari)

Learn and memorize the Divine Attributes of Allah (swt). Choose your sets below to begin studying or quizzing.

How to Use

1. Learn

On the home page, select Learn, choose one or more sets, and press Start Learning! You'll see each Arabic Name, its transliteration, and its primary English meaning.

2. Practice

Select Practice, choose your sets, and hit Start Quiz!

  • Type the English meaning or title for each Divine Name and press Enter.
  • Correct answers turn green and move you directly to the next card.
  • Incorrect attempts will reveal the accepted meaning. If your answer was acceptable, click ✓ I was right to override.
  • Hit Finish Quiz when done to check your score and practice any missed names.

Tips

  • Learn in chunks at a time: don't rush yourself.
  • Slight typographical errors (1 edit distance) are forgiven.
  • The transliteration note (e.g., "Ar-Rahman") is displayed to assist with recognition.

Projected Milestones

Preserving the Names (Iḥṣā')

Scholars note that preserving the Names includes knowing their pronunciation, understanding their meanings, praising and calling upon Allah (swt) through them, and embodying their virtues in daily life.

About

99 Names of Allah is an open-source memorization tool modeled after low-friction spaced repetition quizzes, which I found to be the most effective way of memorizing and retaining information.

It was built alongside the 85% Words of Qur'an app to assist Muslims worldwide in reflecting on Allah's Divine Names and Attributes with ease and consistency.

For more information about the creator of this site, the GitHub repo and contact email, please go to the main page here. We hope this site was helpful!
